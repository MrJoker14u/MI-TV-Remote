Mi TV BT Remote
1. Android Studio me Open > MiTvRemoteBT. Build APK, phone me install.
2. App kholo, Bluetooth permissions Allow karo. Upar "Ready" dikhna chahiye.
3. "Discoverable" dabao (phone 5 min visible rahega).
4. TV: Settings > Remotes & Accessories > Add accessory. List me phone ka naam aayega, usko chuno.
5. Pair hone ke baad app me "TV Chuno" dabao, apna TV select karo. Connected dikhe to remote chalu.
